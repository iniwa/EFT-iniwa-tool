It must be separate from the existing numbered phase/checklist completion system: do not infer phase order, mark a chapter fully complete from the captured subset, migrate/backfill progress, or automatically satisfy ordinary-task gates. Old cash-entry objectives need omission or historical labelling before exposure, not quiet conversion into new game requirements. The current source is not enough for a complete current chapter graph. No new persistent story IDs are allocated in this slice.
